1. Who we are & scope

Shenzhen Yingbei Network Technology Co., LTD ("Yingbei," "we," "us," or "our") is responsible for the personal information it processes in connection with its games and applications. Where applicable data protection law uses the term, we are the data controller for that processing.

This Policy applies to Arrow Melody and other games and applications published by us that display or link to this Policy (each an "App," together the "Apps"), and to related customer support. It covers versions distributed through Google Play, Apple's App Store, and HUAWEI AppGallery, where available.

Features and SDKs differ between Apps, operating systems, distribution channels, regions, and versions. A service described below is used only where it is included in the App you use. An App-specific notice identifies any additional or different processing before it occurs. The App's store privacy disclosures and in-app notices provide further information about that App.

This Policy explains our practices; reading it or accepting our User Agreement does not by itself give consent to optional analytics, personalized advertising, or tracking.

3. Purposes & legal bases

We use information to provide gameplay and requested platform features, manage game progress and purchases, answer support requests, measure and improve game quality, deliver and measure advertising, prevent fraud and abuse, and meet legal obligations.

Where the GDPR, UK GDPR, or similar rules require a legal basis, we rely on:

  • Performance of a contract: information objectively needed to provide the game features, purchases, and support you request.
  • Consent: optional analytics, advertising identifiers, personalized advertising, tracking, and device storage or access where consent is required. GameAnalytics collection is enabled only after the consent required by its service terms and applicable law.
  • Legitimate interests: proportionate security, fraud prevention, and service maintenance where permitted and where your rights do not override those interests. This does not replace consent where consent is required.
  • Legal obligations: applicable accounting, tax, consumer protection, and lawful disclosure requirements.

Declining information needed for a requested purchase or platform feature can prevent that feature from working. Optional analytics or advertising consent is separate from access to unrelated core gameplay.

5. How information is shared

We disclose relevant information to the service categories identified above to perform their stated functions. Scores, nicknames, avatars, or achievements can be visible to other players when you use public leaderboards or platform sharing features. Check the relevant platform's visibility settings before participating.

We can also disclose limited information where required by law or a valid legal request, to protect rights and safety or investigate fraud, or as part of a merger, acquisition, or business transfer with appropriate safeguards and any required notice.

We do not sell personal and sensitive user data for money. Where advertising-related disclosure is considered a "sale," "sharing," or targeted advertising under applicable US state law, the choices in Sections 6 and 9 apply. A non-personalized ad can still involve limited data processing for delivery, security, or measurement.

6. Advertising, permissions & consent

Where consent is required, optional SDK processing starts only after an affirmative choice. Advertising and analytics choices are presented separately from acceptance of general terms. You can withdraw consent through the App's privacy or consent controls, where provided, or contact us. Withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

  • iOS / iPadOS: manage the App's tracking permission in your device privacy settings. We request Apple's App Tracking Transparency authorization before tracking as defined by Apple and do not use alternative identifiers to bypass a refusal.
  • Android / Huawei devices: use the available advertising ID, ad personalization, and privacy settings. Names and available controls depend on the operating system and device.
  • Ad provider controls: an ad's privacy or information icon can provide provider-specific choices. These choices can apply only to that provider and are additional to the App's controls.
  • Platform accounts: manage game visibility, app authorization, and sign-in preferences through HUAWEI ID, Google Play Games, or Game Center settings. Platform sign-out or revocation is separate from deleting information already held by us.
  • Device permissions: review or revoke permissions in your device settings. Denying a permission can affect the feature that needs it. Resetting an advertising identifier or uninstalling an App does not necessarily delete server-side data.

Declining personalized advertising may result in contextual ads. Disabling tracking does not automatically disable all analytics or necessary technical processing; each use must have its own valid basis and respect your choices.

7. Retention & security

We keep personal information only for as long as needed for the stated purpose, taking into account the App's features, your requests, applicable legal duties, and the following criteria:

  • Local progress and settings: remain until reset or removed from the device; device or platform backups can have separate retention.
  • Account-linked progress: retained while needed to provide the linked features, then deleted or anonymized when no longer needed or following a valid deletion request, subject to lawful exceptions.
  • Analytics and advertising data: retained for the period needed for reporting, service improvement, attribution, and fraud prevention, using the relevant service's configured retention and deletion controls. Retention differs by provider and configuration; provider notices explain their independent retention practices.
  • Support, transactions, and compliance: retained as needed to resolve the matter, maintain purchase entitlements, meet tax or accounting duties, resolve disputes, or establish legal claims. Records retained under an exception are restricted to that purpose.

We use appropriate technical and organizational measures, including encrypted network transmission, access restrictions, and limiting data access to authorized personnel and providers. No system can guarantee absolute security. When data is deleted from active systems, residual backups are restricted and removed through the applicable backup lifecycle; any legally required records remain subject to this Policy.

8. Delete accounts & game data

Request deletion from Yingbei. Visit our Account and Data Deletion page or email yingbeiwangluo@163.com with the subject "Yingbei Account and Data Deletion Request." Include the App name, for example Arrow Melody, and your player ID or linked platform if you know it. You do not need to reinstall the App or make a payment to submit a request.

We request only information reasonably needed to locate your records and verify your authority. Do not send a platform password or complete payment card details. Deletion covers the relevant Yingbei account, if any, and associated personal information under our control; we also instruct processors holding that information for us to delete it, subject to applicable legal exceptions.

You can request deletion of an App account, where one exists, and the associated data we control, or request deletion of particular data without deleting the account. A temporary suspension, signing out, or simply unlinking a platform account does not delete those records. If your App offers a direct in-app deletion control, you can use that control as well. Our external request channel remains available when you cannot access the App.

We aim to complete a verified deletion request within 30 days. We respond within the time required by applicable law, normally within one month of receipt where GDPR applies, and explain any lawful extension, necessary verification, expected completion date, and retained records. A shorter legal deadline takes priority. If no identifiable record can be located, we explain that result rather than creating a new tracking identifier. We confirm the outcome when the request has been processed.

Deleting our game data does not delete your Google Account, HUAWEI ID, Apple Account, or independently held platform records. For data held by a platform, use its privacy controls; Google Play Games also provides game-data deletion through the Play Games profile. Deleting a platform record does not necessarily remove a separate copy held by us, so contact us for that copy.

Deletion may permanently remove saved progress. Some purchase records may need to be retained for legal duties or purchase support. Deleting an account or uninstalling an App does not automatically cancel a store subscription; manage subscriptions with the store. Subscription cancellation is not a condition for requesting immediate deletion.

9. Your privacy rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you can ask to access or obtain a copy of your information, correct inaccuracies, delete information, restrict or object to processing, receive portable data, or withdraw consent. You can also complain to the data protection authority responsible for your location.

Residents of applicable US states may also request information about collection and disclosure, opt out of sale, sharing, or targeted advertising, and appeal a denied request. To appeal, reply to our response or contact us with the subject "Privacy Appeal." An authorized agent may submit a request with appropriate proof of authority. We do not unlawfully discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

Use the App's available privacy controls or the contact in Section 13. We verify requests proportionately and explain any lawful limitation. Where applicable law requires us to honor a recognized universal opt-out signal, we do so for the processing and environment in which that signal is received.

Advertising providers can use permitted activity data to infer interests for ad selection. We do not use personal information to make solely automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about you.

10. International processing

We are based in China. Our service providers operate internationally, so personal information can be processed outside your country, including in China, the United States, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Singapore, and other locations where the relevant provider operates, depending on service and configuration. Those countries may have different privacy laws.

Where a transfer is subject to restrictions, we use a legally permitted mechanism appropriate to that transfer, such as an adequacy decision or applicable standard contractual clauses and required supplementary safeguards. Contact us to ask about the destination and safeguards relevant to your information or to request a copy of applicable safeguards, with confidential details redacted where necessary.

11. Children & younger users

Our Apps are general-audience casual games, including puzzle, solitaire, and block puzzle games. They are not specifically directed to children under 13. A store content rating does not itself determine the legal age for personal-data consent.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, or below the applicable local age requiring parental consent, without the authorization and safeguards required by law. We do not knowingly direct personalized advertising or tracking to children where prohibited. When a younger user's age becomes known, the applicable age restrictions, consent requirements, and SDK protections must apply.

If you believe a child has provided information contrary to this Policy, contact us with the App name and enough information to locate the record. We will investigate and delete the information or take other steps required by law. A parent or guardian can exercise applicable rights on the child's behalf.

12. Changes to this Policy

We update this Policy when our practices or legal requirements change and revise the date above. Material changes are communicated through an appropriate in-app notice or other suitable channel. When a new use requires consent, we obtain that consent before starting the use; continued use alone does not provide it.
