1. Submit a deletion request

This page applies to Arrow Melody and other Apps published by Shenzhen Yingbei Network Technology Co., LTD that link to our Privacy Policy. You can request deletion even if you have uninstalled the App, stopped using a platform account, or do not have a separate Yingbei account.

Send a request to yingbeiwangluo@163.com with the subject Yingbei Account and Data Deletion Request. Include:

  • The name of the App, for example Arrow Melody.
  • Your game or player ID, if known. If it is unavailable, describe the platform and any information that can reasonably help locate your records.
  • Whether you want to delete an App account and associated data, where an account exists, or only specific personal information.
  • Your country or region, so we can apply the relevant privacy rules.

Prepare deletion request

This button opens your email app with a request template. Review and send the email to submit your request; opening the template does not submit it. If no email app is configured, send the request from your usual email service to the address above. This webpage does not collect or submit form data.

No purchase or App reinstall is needed. Do not send your platform password, authentication codes, full card details, or unrelated identity documents.

2. What is deleted

For a verified account-deletion request, we delete the relevant App account, if one exists, and associated personal information under our control, including linked identifiers, profile information, synchronized progress, and other identifiable game records that are not subject to a lawful retention exception. Deactivation, freezing, or unlinking alone is not deletion.

We also instruct service providers processing the relevant information on our behalf to delete it. You may request deletion of specific data without closing an account. We explain if a requested deletion prevents us from providing a feature that requires the information.

We can only act on records we can locate and are authorized to control. Some information is held only on your device, or independently by a platform or advertising provider. The sections below explain those cases.

3. Information that may be retained

Limited records may be retained where required or permitted by applicable law for tax or accounting, purchase verification, fraud prevention, security, or legal claims. We identify any applicable exception and the retention period or criteria in our response. Retained records are restricted to the relevant purpose.

Residual backup copies are restricted and removed through the applicable backup lifecycle. Information that has already been irreversibly anonymized so that it can no longer identify you is not personal information and may be retained as aggregate statistics. Pseudonymous identifiers are not treated as anonymous merely because they do not contain your name.

4. Verification & timing

We may ask for a proportionate verification step to prevent another person from deleting your data. We use only the information reasonably needed for verification and record matching. Not knowing your player ID does not prevent you from contacting us.

We aim to complete a verified deletion request within 30 days. We respond within the deadline set by applicable law, generally within one month of receipt for GDPR requests. A shorter legal deadline takes priority. If a lawful extension is needed, we explain the reason and expected completion date within the original response period.

We confirm completion or explain any limitation, such as a legal retention requirement or no identifiable records being found. If you disagree with a decision, reply with the subject Privacy Appeal. You may also contact the appropriate data protection authority.

5. HUAWEI ID, Google Play Games & Game Center

Deleting Yingbei-held game data does not close your HUAWEI ID, Google Account, Apple Account, or independently maintained platform profile. You do not need to close one of those platform accounts to request deletion from us.

  • Google Play Games: Google provides game-data controls through your Play Games profile. See Google's game-profile and game-data deletion instructions.
  • HUAWEI ID: review linked-app authorizations and privacy rights through your account settings and the HUAWEI ID Privacy Statement.
  • Apple Game Center: manage your profile and sharing in Game Center settings. Apple's Game Center Privacy Notice explains information held by Apple and your controls.

Revoking a platform authorization or deleting platform-held information does not necessarily delete a separate copy held by Yingbei. Use the email request above for information under our control. Provider-specific privacy requests can also be made through the notices in our SDK disclosure table.

6. Local progress, purchases & subscriptions

Data stored only on your device can be removed using an available in-App reset, the operating system's App-data controls, or by deleting the App and its local data. Offloading an App, keeping a platform backup, or reinstalling may preserve or restore some records, depending on your device and settings. Server-side data requires a separate deletion request.

Account or game-data deletion can permanently remove progress and may affect access to saved content. It does not automatically cancel a subscription or guarantee a purchase refund. Manage subscription renewal through the store that billed you. You can request immediate deletion without first canceling a subscription, but billing can continue unless you separately cancel renewal.

Read our retention policy and subscription and refund terms for further information.
